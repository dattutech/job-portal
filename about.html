Our story

Built to get you hired, faster

JobLane started as a weekend project to fix one problem: job boards that bury real openings under noise.

Why JobLane

Every listing verified, every update automatic

We started JobLane because scrolling through outdated listings and broken apply links was wasting hours for freshers who needed that time to prepare. Every posting here is pulled from a single structured feed, so when a new role goes live, it appears across the site — home, category pages, and search — instantly and consistently.

We focus heavily on the freshers segment: campus hiring drives, internships, government notifications, and entry-level tech roles that traditional boards often deprioritise.
